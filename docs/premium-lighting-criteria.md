# Premium Lighting Criteria

A checklist for what separates cheap LED lighting from lighting that just *feels* better — the source, the fixture, and the interaction.

---

## Light quality (the source)

- **High CRI (90+, ideally 95+)** — colors look natural; skin, wood, and food look right rather than sallow or grey.
- **Good R9 value (deep red)** — reds stay rich instead of muddy; the detail behind "food looks appetizing."
- **Smooth, full spectrum** — no harsh blue spike or gappy dips; closer to sunlight/blackbody.
- **Warm color temperature (2700–3000K)** for living spaces — cozy rather than clinical.
- **Dim-to-warm** — shifts amber as it dims, mimicking incandescent; a very premium touch.
- **Low flicker** — well-filtered drivers; no eye strain, no strobing on moving hands.
- **Tight binning consistency** — every fixture matches; no one downlight looks pink and the next green.

## The fixture / install (hiding the source)

- **Continuous line of light** — dense LEDs or COB strip; no visible "dotting," especially in reflections.
- **Diffuser / aluminum profile** — frosted lens spreads the light, hides the diodes, doubles as heatsink for stable color.
- **No visible source / glare control** — deep baffles, louvers, good optics; you see the *lit surface*, not the raw LED.
- **Smart placement** — front lip of cabinets, aimed to light the surface evenly, no self-shadow, no source in your eyeline.
- **Smooth deep dimming** — flicker-free down to ~1%; no dropout, buzz, or stutter at low levels.

## The interaction (motion design of the light)

- **Fade in/out, not on/off** — the space *responds* instead of a machine snapping.
- **Eased timing curve (ease-in-out)** — starts slow, accelerates, settles; matches how physical things move. *The hardest-to-name, most important one.*
- **Tuned latency (~150–300ms)** — responsive but with a little "weight"; not sluggish, not twitchy.
- **Clean, consistent trigger** — quality sensor fires the same way every time, at the right point in travel.
- **Warm settle on fade-out** — shifts slightly warmer as it closes, like an ember.

## The meta-principle

- **Intentionality in throwaway moments** — someone cared about a two-second interaction nobody consciously notices (the fork drawer). That care *is* what "premium" means.
